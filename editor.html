Character editor

A skeleton with generated skin, built from each character's measurements. The body is modelled as a smooth signed-distance field and turned into a mesh once per measurement change. After that it's posed by GPU skinning on the skeleton.

Pose editor

Freezes the discipline scene at the current beat so you can reshape it. Drag a joint in the view; click one to fine-tune it below. The gold handle moves the implement, and the arm follows. Report collects everything for Claude. The scene goes back to its own pose when you stop editing.

Report — to make an edit permanent

Everything here is a preview: nothing is saved, and the game never sees it. To keep an edit, make a report, copy it and paste it back into the chat, and it will be made permanent in the project.
Try a design in the preview: a few fields as JSON ({"height":172,"outfit":{"hair":"#5a1e12"}}) or a whole preset as “Show as JSON” prints it. Objects merge, arrays replace, null removes a field; colours can be numbers, 0xrrggbb or "#rrggbb".
